    Loss Estimation#

    The loss estimation module turns the damage-state probability library into expected loss ratios, building-by-event loss ratios, expected annual loss (EAL) and zone aggregates. It consumes the STARR-compatible export of the Exposure module (M, C, continuous hazard IDs, building values and zone ids) together with the annual event probabilities.

    Shared inputs of the loss-estimation chain.

    Building Loss#

    building_loss #

    Building-by-event loss ratios and expected annual loss.

    Continuous hazard IDs from the exposure module are mapped onto lookup-table bins with a selectable index convention, combined across wind and flood, and weighted by the annual event probabilities to give each building's expected annual loss (EAL). Large exports are processed in row chunks so the (n_buildings, n_events) arrays can stay memory-mapped on disk.

    Lookup Tables#

    lookup_tables #

    Expected loss-ratio lookup tables derived from the damage library.

    For every archetype m and configuration c the damage library holds a probability distribution over the damage states for each (wind ID, flood ID) pair. Multiplying those probabilities by the damage-state ratios gives the expected loss ratio. The marginal tables L_mcw_wd (wind only, flood at the zero-hazard ID) and L_mcf_fld (flood only, wind at the zero-hazard ID) are the STARR-compatible artefacts of the verified workflow; the optional full surface keeps the joint wind-flood interaction.

    Compute the wind-only and flood-only expected loss-ratio tables.

    wind[m-1, c-1, w] = sum_d P[m, c][w, 0, d] * ratios[d] holds flood at its zero-hazard ID; flood[m-1, c-1, f] = sum_d P[m, c][0, f, d] * ratios[d] holds wind at its zero-hazard ID.
